Photon — Cyberpunk Neon Variants

Six takes on the Carbon Bar skeleton you love — same streak, accent-bar selection, mono query, slot chips and footer. Each changes only the palette (and glow). One will become the Neon theme. All stay dark, polished and lightweight.

Violet glass · cyan accent · magenta streak

The balanced one. Deep violet-black, neon cyan for UI chrome and selection glow, hot magenta for the streak trail and calculator results. Mono query. Reads as cyberpunk without shouting.
