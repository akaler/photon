Photon — Neon City POC v2

Three compositions of the same idea: a dense forest of skyscrapers in the distance (two depth layers) and a foreground neon sign that owns the frame. Same 90×58 pixel grid, same 15fps engine, procedurally generated — hit ↻ regenerate on each.

V1 · Dense Harbour

Skyline forest + LED ticker sign

Two distance layers — a hazy far row and a denser mid row — fill the whole width. A very tall tower on the right carries a framed LED ticker sign: the glyph dots scroll sideways like a stock ticker, re-rolling as they go. One dying bar sign buzzes on a mid tower. Beacon on the tallest spire.
